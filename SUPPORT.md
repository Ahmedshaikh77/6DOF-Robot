# Support

For help understanding the committed model, documentation, or historical artifacts, open the issue form that best matches the question:

- [Bug report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md) for an observed defect in a tracked source or asset.
- [Reproducibility report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/reproducibility.md) for clean-workspace Xacro, build, or launch results.
- [Documentation report](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/documentation.md) for inaccurate, unclear, or unsupported wording.

Include your ROS distribution, operating system, clean-workspace context, exact command, complete relevant output, and observed versus expected behavior. For new meshes or screenshots, include asset provenance.

Support is limited to the committed portfolio materials. It does not cover hardware integration, robot control, motion planning, safety certification, or physical deployment.

# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss model, documentation, and reproduction evidence in good faith.
- Respect differing technical experience and perspectives.
- Keep criticism specific to the work and offer actionable alternatives when possible.
- Protect private information and do not publish credentials, personal data, or sensitive environment details.

## Reporting

No verified private conduct-reporting email is published. To request a private channel, open a minimal public issue titled `Private report channel request`. Include no sensitive, vulnerability, or incident details in that issue. The repository owner can then establish a private channel for the conduct report. Reports will be reviewed with discretion, and appropriate action may include clarification, a warning, removal of content, or restriction of participation.

This code applies to project spaces and related interactions.
